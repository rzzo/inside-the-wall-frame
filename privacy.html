Privacy policy

Inside the Wall frame is a private wall display used by one family in their own home. Last updated 29 October 2026.

What it accesses

Read-only access to Google Calendar events (calendar.events.readonly), granted by the family's own Google account. It cannot create, change or delete anything.

How the data is used

  • Calendar events are used only to show the family's schedule on their picture frame at home.
  • The sign-in and the events are kept on one small computer in the family's home. They are not sold, shared, or sent to anyone else. There are no ads, analytics or tracking.
  • Only the family's own Google account signs in. The app is not offered to anyone else.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time in the Google Account: Security → Third-party apps & services.

Google user data

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
